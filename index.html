02 / On a phone

Most of your customers are on a phone.

So every site is designed at phone width first and then built up. Buttons stay thumb-sized, text stays readable, and nothing needs pinching or sideways scrolling.

  • No sideways scrolling on any screen
  • Menu that opens and closes properly on a phone
  • Big tap targets on every button and form field
  • Checked on a real narrow screen, not just resized
